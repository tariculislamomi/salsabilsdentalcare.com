Does getting a root canal hurt?

With modern anaesthesia techniques, root canal treatment is no more uncomfortable than a routine filling. Most patients report feeling nothing during the procedure. Any mild soreness afterwards typically resolves within 1–2 days.

How soon can I book an appointment?

You can book same-day or next-day appointments via the contact form below or by calling us directly. We also offer video consultations for initial advice before your in-person visit.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. Our clinic is family-friendly and we have extensive experience making dental visits fun and fear-free for children of all ages, from first check-ups to orthodontic evaluations.

What is mercury-free dentistry?

Traditional silver amalgam fillings contain mercury. We exclusively use composite resin and ceramic materials — equally durable, tooth-coloured, and free from any toxic metals. Safer for you, and the environment.
